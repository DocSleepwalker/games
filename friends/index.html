Friends: The One With The Embarrassing Trivia

A small game inspired by my favorite TV show «Friends» and one of its most memorable episodes — «The One With the Embryos» (S04E12). Explore the apartments of our favorite characters and take part in the famous trivia game prepared by Ross.
🎮 Controls: Arrow keys — move, Z / X — action (select/interact).
⭐ Features:
  • Choose who to play as: Chandler or Monica
  • Explore the apartments to find clues
  • Interactions and opinions depend on the character
  • Different trivia questions for Chandler and Monica
  • Theme music from the show in the main menu
  • Hidden ending
Credits: Based on a modified platformer engine by taxicomics.
